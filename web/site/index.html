Product

Everything you need to help, nothing that works behind someone's back

Remote ID

Each registered device gets a 9-digit ID, like 105 280 964. An ID only lets someone ask to connect — it is never a password.

Consent on the target

Every session needs approval on the remote screen, showing who is asking and what they want to do. No answer means no access.

How it works

Four steps, with the other person in charge

  1. 1

    Install and sign in

    Install the Windows app and sign in with the account your administrator invited.

  2. 2

    Share your Remote ID

    The person you are helping reads you the ID shown on their Home screen.

  3. 3

    They approve

    They see your name and the access you asked for, and choose Accept or Reject.

  4. 4

    Work together

    A banner shows the session is active. Either side can disconnect instantly.

Security

Designed so access is always visible and always yours to end

Protect yourself from support scams

Only accept a request from someone you know. Never accept a request from someone who contacted you unexpectedly claiming to be support.

  • Explicit consent. No session starts without approval on the remote screen; there is no hidden or unattended mode.
  • Encryption. HTTPS and secure WebSockets for sign-in and signalling; screen data travels peer to peer with DTLS-SRTP.
  • Your screen stays yours. Our servers broker the connection but never receive or store screen content.
  • Strong accounts. Invitation-only sign-up, Argon2id password hashing, short-lived tokens and instant revocation.
  • Audit trail. Every request, approval and session end is recorded for administrators.
  • Least privilege. Each service gets only the permissions it needs, and secrets are never logged.
